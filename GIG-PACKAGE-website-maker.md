## Why this gig is winnable for a beginner

- Local shops (cafés, coaching classes, salons, hardware stores, clinics) mostly have NO website — only a Facebook page or nothing
- They don't want "web development", they want "my shop on the internet" — the demo shows exactly that
- ₹2,500 is below agency pricing (₹10k+) and above "my nephew will do it free" credibility zone
- Build time with AI assistance: 2–4 hours per client → strong hourly rate even at low price
